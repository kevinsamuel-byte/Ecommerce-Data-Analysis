# E-commerce / Restaurant Data Analysis

## Objective
To analyze transactional data and extract insights on customer spending behavior, tipping patterns, and factors influencing total bill amounts.

---

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

---

## Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Univariate analysis (distribution, outliers)
- Bivariate analysis (relationships between variables)
- Correlation analysis (heatmap)
- Feature engineering (tip percentage calculation)

---

## Key Insights
- Spending is higher on weekends, especially Sunday, indicating increased customer activity
- Dinner sessions generate higher revenue compared to lunch, likely due to social dining behavior
- Larger group sizes tend to produce higher total bills, though variability exists within groups
- Strong positive relationship between total bill and tip amount
- Tip percentage is generally consistent between 10% and 20%, with a few high-value outliers

---

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights clear patterns in customer behavior, including higher spending during weekends and dinner sessions, as well as consistent tipping habits. These insights can help businesses optimize pricing strategies and customer targeting.
